Most rules mistakes at casual tables are about **what you can do once per turn** and **what the first player is not allowed to do**. This guide covers both.

## Setting up the game

1. Shake hands, then flip a coin. The winner of the flip chooses who goes first.
2. Each player shuffles their 60-card deck and draws 7 cards.
3. Each player puts one Basic Pokémon face down as their Active Pokémon and up to 5 more Basic Pokémon face down on their Bench.
4. If a player has no Basic Pokémon, they reveal their hand, shuffle it back and draw 7 again (a mulligan). For each extra mulligan, the opponent may draw one more card.
5. Each player sets aside the top 6 cards of their deck face down as Prize cards.
6. Both players flip their Pokémon face up and the first turn begins.

## The three parts of a turn

### 1. Draw a card

Every turn starts by drawing one card. If you cannot draw because your deck is empty, you lose the game.

### 2. Take actions

You may do any of the following, in any order:

- **Put Basic Pokémon onto your Bench** (any number, up to 5 Benched Pokémon).
- **Evolve** your Pokémon (any number), but not a Pokémon that came into play this turn.
- **Attach one Energy card** from your hand to one of your Pokémon. This is limited to **once per turn**.
- **Play Item cards** (any number).
- **Play one Supporter card**. This is limited to **once per turn**.
- **Play one Stadium card** if there is not already one with the same name in play. Limited to once per turn.
- **Retreat** your Active Pokémon once per turn by discarding Energy equal to its Retreat Cost.
- **Use Abilities**, following the text on each card. Many say "once during your turn".

### 3. Attack

Attacking ends your turn. Check that your Active Pokémon has the Energy the attack needs, apply damage (see [How Damage Is Calculated](../damage-calculation/)), and check for Knock Outs. Then Pokémon Checkup happens before the next player's turn.

## First-turn rules

- The player who goes **first cannot attack** on their first turn.
- The player who goes **first cannot play a Supporter card** on their first turn.
- **Neither player can evolve** on their own first turn.

## Once-per-game effects

- **VSTAR Power**: each player can use only one VSTAR Power per game, no matter how many Pokémon VSTAR they have.
- **GX attacks**: in formats where they are legal, each player can use only one GX attack per game.
- **ACE SPEC cards**: a deck can contain only one ACE SPEC card in total.

## Using PTCG Companion for these rules

The **Energy**, **Supporter**, **Retreat Used** and **Ability** toggles on each side are reminders for the once-per-turn actions above. Tap **Turn ➔** at the end of a turn and they all reset. Tap **P1 First** or **P2 First** at the start so both players remember who has the first-turn restrictions. The VSTAR, GX and ACE SPEC buttons dim once used and stay that way until you start a new match.
