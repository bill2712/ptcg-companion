PTCG Companion replaces the dice, damage counters, coins and condition markers you would normally carry to a Pokémon TCG match. One phone sits flat between both players. The top half is rotated 180° so your opponent reads their side the right way up, and the bottom half is yours.

## Damage counters

The big red number on each side is the damage on that player's **Active Pokémon**. Use **+10**, **+50** and **+100** to add damage and **−10** to correct a mistake. Every Pokémon TCG attack deals damage in multiples of 10, so one tap is always one damage counter.

When the Active Pokémon is Knocked Out or retreats, tap **Reset** inside the number box to clear it. If you tapped the wrong button, **Undo** steps that side back one action at a time (the last 20 actions are kept).

> Tip: the counter tracks one Pokémon per player, the Active one. Damage on Benched Pokémon is best tracked with physical counters or a note, because those Pokémon do not change as often.

## Prize cards

The six numbered tiles are your Prize cards. Tap a tile when you take a Prize card and it is crossed out. When all six are taken the app announces the winner. Pokémon that give two or three Prize cards just mean tapping two or three tiles. See [Prize Cards and How to Win](../prize-cards-and-winning/) for the full rules.

## Special Conditions

The five buttons at the bottom of each side are **Poison**, **Burn**, **Asleep**, **Paralyzed** and **Confused**. The app follows the official marker rules:

- Poison and Burn can be on a Pokémon at the same time as any other condition.
- Asleep, Paralyzed and Confused replace each other, because in the physical game they are shown by turning the card. Turning on one turns the other two off.

The border of the player area glows in the colour of the active condition, so both players can see it from across the table. [Special Conditions Explained](../special-conditions/) covers what each one does.

## Pokémon Checkup

Tap **Checkup** between turns. The app adds 10 damage to every Poisoned Active Pokémon and 20 damage to every Burned one, and reminds you which coin flips are still needed (Asleep and Burned). If you made a mistake, **Undo** on that side reverts it.

## Once-per-turn reminders

Four small toggles help you remember what you have already done this turn: **Energy** (one attachment from your hand per turn), **Supporter** (one Supporter card per turn), **Retreat Used** and **Ability**. Pressing **Turn ➔** in the centre adds one to the turn counter and clears all four for both players.

**VSTAR**, **GX** and **ACE SPEC** are once-per-game markers. Tap one after you use your VSTAR Power or GX attack and it dims for the rest of the game.

## Type colour

The row of small coloured dots at the top lets you tint your side with your Active Pokémon's type. It is purely visual, but it makes it easy to see at a glance which matchup is in play.

## Coin flip and first player

**Flip coin** gives a fair random result using your device's random number generator. Use **P1 First / P2 First** to record who went first; that matters for first-turn rules described in [Turn Structure and Once-per-Turn Rules](../turn-structure/).

## Match timer

The yellow clock in the middle is the match timer. Tap it to start or pause. Open **☰ Menu** to reset it or pick 25, 30 or 50 minutes. When time runs out the clock turns red and the phone vibrates (if your device supports it).

## Your match is saved automatically

Everything on screen is saved in your browser's local storage after every tap. If the screen locks, you switch apps or the page reloads, the match comes back exactly where you left it. Use **☰ Menu → New match** to clear it. Nothing is uploaded; see our [Privacy Policy](/en/privacy-policy/).

## Add it to your home screen

On iPhone, open the site in Safari, tap Share and choose **Add to Home Screen**. On Android, open the Chrome menu and choose **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**. It then opens full screen like a normal app, and the screen stays awake during a match.
