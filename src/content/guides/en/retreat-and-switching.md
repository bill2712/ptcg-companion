Your Active Pokémon is not stuck there forever. Moving it to the Bench and bringing up a different Pokémon is one of the most useful plays in the game, whether you want to save a damaged Pokémon, get rid of a Special Condition or bring in a better attacker. There are two ways to do it: **retreating** and **switching**.

## Retreating

Retreating is a normal action you can take during your turn, before you attack.

1. Look at the **Retreat Cost** at the bottom right of your Active Pokémon. Each Colorless symbol is one Energy.
2. **Discard that many Energy** from the Active Pokémon. Because the symbols are Colorless, any type of Energy works.
3. Swap the Active Pokémon with one of your Benched Pokémon.

A Pokémon with **no symbols** in its Retreat Cost retreats for free.

### Rules to remember

- You can **retreat only once per turn**.
- The Pokémon you retreat keeps its **damage counters** and any Energy you did not discard.
- Pokémon Tools stay attached.
- You need at least one Benched Pokémon to swap with.
- You can retreat and then still attack with the new Active Pokémon, if it has the Energy it needs.

## Switching with card effects

Many cards move your Active Pokémon to the Bench without paying a Retreat Cost. Common examples are Items that "switch your Active Pokémon with 1 of your Benched Pokémon", and Abilities that move a Pokémon into the Active Spot.

Switching is **not retreating**, which makes a big difference:

- You pay **no Retreat Cost**.
- It **does not use** your once-per-turn retreat, so you can switch and retreat in the same turn.
- It **works on Asleep and Paralyzed Pokémon**, which cannot retreat.

## Special Conditions and the Bench

**Any** move to the Bench, by retreating or switching, **removes all Special Conditions** from that Pokémon. This is the main way to cure Poison, Burn and Confusion.

- **Asleep** and **Paralyzed** Pokémon **cannot retreat**. You need a switching card, an evolution (which also removes conditions) or a heal effect.
- **Confused** Pokémon **can retreat** normally. Retreating is often better than risking the coin flip to attack.
- **Poisoned** and **Burned** Pokémon can retreat normally.

More details are in [Special Conditions explained](/en/guides/special-conditions/).

## When your opponent moves your Pokémon

Some opposing effects, often called "gust" effects, **bring one of your Benched Pokémon into the Active Spot**. Your old Active Pokémon goes to the Bench and loses its Special Conditions. If the new Active Pokémon has a large Retreat Cost and no Energy, it can be stuck there, which is exactly why players use these effects.

## When to retreat

Retreating costs Energy, and Energy is one attachment per turn, so it is a real price. Good reasons to pay it:

- **Save a Pokémon** that is about to be Knocked Out, especially a Pokémon ex that would give up 2 Prize cards.
- **Escape a Special Condition** that would otherwise stop your attack or deal damage in Pokémon Checkup.
- **Bring in the right attacker**, for example one the defending Pokémon is weak to. See [Weakness and Resistance](/en/guides/weakness-and-resistance/).
- **Spread damage**: move a damaged Pokémon to the Bench and heal it later.

And reasons to hold off:

- You would be left without Energy to attack next turn.
- A free switching card in your hand would do the same job.
- The Pokémon you would bring up is even more vulnerable.

## Deck-building tips

- Include a **"pivot"** Pokémon with free or low Retreat Cost. Start with it, then retreat into your main attacker cheaply.
- Play a few **switching Items**. They solve Asleep and Paralyzed, and save Energy.
- Avoid too many Pokémon with a Retreat Cost of 3 or more unless your deck has ways to move them.

The [beginner deck-building guide](/en/guides/how-to-build-a-pokemon-deck/) shows how many switching cards a first deck usually needs.

## Tracking retreat in PTCG Companion

[PTCG Companion](/en/) has a **Retreat Used** toggle for each player, so nobody retreats twice in one turn. It resets when you tap **Turn ➔**. When you retreat or switch, select the Benched Pokémon and tap **⇄** in the app: the damage moves with each Pokémon, and the one that goes to the Bench loses its Special Conditions.
