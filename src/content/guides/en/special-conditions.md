Special Conditions are effects that attacks put on the opponent's **Active Pokémon**. There are five: Poisoned, Burned, Asleep, Paralyzed and Confused. Only an Active Pokémon can have a Special Condition; a Benched Pokémon never does.

## Which conditions can be combined?

In the physical game, Poisoned and Burned are shown with markers placed on the card, while Asleep, Paralyzed and Confused are shown by **turning the card**. Since a card can only face one way, a Pokémon can only be **one** of Asleep, Paralyzed or Confused at a time. A newer one replaces the old one.

Poison and Burn markers are separate, so a Pokémon can be, for example, Poisoned, Burned *and* Asleep at once. PTCG Companion follows the same logic automatically.

## Poisoned

Put 1 damage counter (10 damage) on the Poisoned Pokémon during every Pokémon Checkup. Some attacks say to put more counters than usual; in that case use the amount the card says.

## Burned

During every Pokémon Checkup, put 2 damage counters (20 damage) on the Burned Pokémon. Then its owner flips a coin. If heads, the Pokémon recovers and is no longer Burned.

## Asleep

An Asleep Pokémon **cannot attack or retreat**. During every Pokémon Checkup its owner flips a coin. If heads, it wakes up.

## Paralyzed

A Paralyzed Pokémon **cannot attack or retreat**. It recovers automatically during the Pokémon Checkup after its owner's next turn. In practice, Paralysis takes away one full turn.

## Confused

A Confused Pokémon can still retreat. When it tries to attack, its owner flips a coin first. If tails, put 3 damage counters on the Confused Pokémon and the attack does not happen (the turn still ends).

## Pokémon Checkup

Pokémon Checkup happens **between every turn**, after each player's turn ends, for both players' Active Pokémon. Conditions are handled in this order:

1. Poisoned
2. Burned
3. Asleep
4. Paralyzed

After that, check whether any Pokémon has been Knocked Out. The **Checkup** button in PTCG Companion applies the Poison and Burn damage for both sides in one tap and reminds you which flips you still need.

## How to remove Special Conditions

All Special Conditions are removed when the Pokémon:

- moves to the Bench (by retreating, or by a card that switches it out),
- evolves, or
- is affected by a card that says it recovers from Special Conditions.

Remember that Asleep and Paralyzed Pokémon cannot retreat, so you need a switching card or an evolution to get them out.

## Quick reference

| Condition | Can attack? | Can retreat? | At Checkup |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Poisoned | Yes | Yes | +10 damage |
| Burned | Yes | Yes | +20 damage, then flip: heads recovers |
| Asleep | No | No | Flip: heads wakes up |
| Paralyzed | No | No | Recovers after owner's next turn |
| Confused | Flip first | Yes | — |
